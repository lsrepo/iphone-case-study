# Payment methods (Hong Kong)

| Payment method | Implemented? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Card | ✅ Yes | — |
| Google Pay | ✅ Yes | — |
| Apple Pay | ⚠️ Code-ready, not live | Apple Developer account required (external, ~$99/yr) |
| WeChat Pay | ⚠️ Code-ready, not live | Sandbox account not enabled; also requires webhook |
| Alipay | ⚠️ Code-ready, not live | Sandbox account not enabled; also requires webhook |
| AlipayHK | ❌ No | Sandbox account not enabled; also requires webhook |
| PayPal | ❌ No | Sandbox account not enabled |
| Octopus | ❌ No | Sandbox account not enabled (Checkout.com Beta) |
| UnionPay (card scheme) | ❌ No | Sandbox account not enabled |
| FPS | ❌ No | Not offered by Checkout.com |
| PayMe | ❌ No | Not offered by Checkout.com |

"Sandbox account not enabled" means enabling it requires Checkout.com
Dashboard access, which this environment doesn't have.
